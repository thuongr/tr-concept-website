# Registration & Enquiry Rules

Core rule: save the business record before attempting email.

## Enquiry
Validate → find/create Contact → Form Submission → confirmation email → Email Log.

## Course
Contact → Enrolment → Form Submission → optional marketing consent → acknowledgement email → manual cohort assignment.

## Community
Contact → Community Registration → Form Submission → optional marketing consent → confirmation email → manual attendance.

Detailed class scheduling and FB/Zalo group setup remain human-managed.
